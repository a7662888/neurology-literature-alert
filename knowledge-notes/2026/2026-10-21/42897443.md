## 核心發現（Key Findings）

最終研究世代納入129名MSA患者(76名MSA-C、53名MSA-P)與184名HC。基線時萎縮最明顯的結構在MSA-C為小腦下幕區(infratentorial regions)，在MSA-P為殼核(putamen)。兩亞型皆觀察到小腦下幕結構相較HC呈現最大之縱向容積減少。小腦-橋腦-殼核體積指數之年化變化為MSA患者中效果量最大的進展生物標記(效果量: -1.81 [95% CI: -2.14, -1.55])，於兩亞型中亦然(MSA-C: -2.04 [95% CI: -2.58, -1.68]；MSA-P: -1.55 [95% CI: -2.08, -1.22])，且偵測30%治療效果所需樣本數最低(MSA每組N=55)，表現優於臨床量表(每組所需患者數減少59.6%)。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Recent studies have reported longitudinal brain atrophy in multiple system atrophy (MSA) in relatively small patient cohorts.

OBJECTIVE: To compare longitudinal regional brain volume and clinical changes as endpoint measures for MSA clinical trials.

METHODS: MSA patients with 1-year longitudinal brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) were selected from the placebo arm of the M-STAR Study (NCT03952806) and stratified into MSA-cerebellar (MSA-C) and MSA-parkinsonism (MSA-P) subtypes. A multisite healthy control (HC) cohort from international datasets was included for comparison. Baseline and longitudinal atrophy were assessed through automated MRI volumetry. Standardized longitudinal effect size values with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) and sample sizes required to detect treatment effect were estimated for 42 individual regional brain volumes, four combined volumetric indexes, nine clinical scales, and serum neurofilament light chain.

RESULTS: The final study cohort included 129 MSA patients (76 MSA-C, 53 MSA-P) and 184 HC. The most atrophic structures at baseline were infratentorial regions in MSA-C and putamen in MSA-P. In both subtypes, infratentorial structures showed the largest longitudinal volume loss compared with HC. The annualized change of a cerebellum-pons-putamen volumetric index was the most powerful progression biomarker in MSA patients (effect size: -1.81 [95% CI: -2.14, -1.55]) and in both subtypes (MSA-C: -2.04 [95% CI: -2.58, -1.68], MSA-P: -1.55 [95% CI: -2.08, -1.22]), requiring the lowest sample size per arm (MSA, N = 55 to detect 30% treatment effect), and outperforming clinical measures (-59.6% patients required per arm).

CONCLUSION: These findings support longitudinal MRI-derived regional atrophy rates as sensitive imaging endpoints to assess treatment efficacy in MSA clinical trials. © 2026 The Author(s).
