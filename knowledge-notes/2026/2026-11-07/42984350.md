## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

觀察到周邊感覺神經系統異常在SCA3中相當普遍，且可能於運動失調症狀出現前即已存在，並與疾病嚴重度相關，提示感覺神經退化可能是SCA3中運動失調表現的一個被低估的來源。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

此為橫斷面觀察性研究，顯示神經電生理與超音波測量可能具有作為SCA3周邊神經退化生物標記的潛力，但因果關係與長期變化趨勢無法由本研究確立。

## 教學應用（Teaching Application）

可用於教學說明SCA3不僅影響中樞小腦系統，周邊感覺神經系統（包括背根神經節）亦可能早期受累，並展示結合電生理與影像學工具評估周邊神經病變的研究方法。

## 批判（Strengths & Limitations）

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Postmortem studies have shown significant degeneration of the peripheral nervous system (PNS) in spinocerebellar ataxia type 3 (SCA3). Although PNS involvement accelerates overall ataxia progression, PNS biomarkers for therapeutic trials are lacking and our mechanistic understanding of related clinical manifestations remains incomplete.

OBJECTIVE: We employed a multimodal approach to characterize and quantify peripheral sensory nervous system degeneration throughout the course of SCA3.

METHODS: Forty SCA3 mutation carriers, including 10 preataxic individuals, were enrolled in this study. Detailed clinical assessments of ataxia and neuropathy severity were combined with high-resolution nerve ultrasound and electrophysiological examinations to evaluate cross-sectional areas (CSAs) and sensory action potential amplitudes of upper and lower limb nerves.

RESULTS: Sensory axonal loss in at least one nerve was found in 97% of ataxic and 50% of preataxic individuals. Ultrasound examinations showed CSA reductions at one or more measurement sites in at least two nerves in all participants. Upper limb nerves were more commonly affected than the sural nerve, which supports a widespread, non-length-dependent pattern of axonal damage. The degree of sensory axonal loss was associated with ataxia severity, estimated disease duration, and patient-reported ability to perform activities of daily living.

CONCLUSIONS: Abnormalities of the peripheral sensory nervous system are highly prevalent in SCA3, emerge before ataxia onset, and reflect disease severity. The combination of electrophysiological and ultrasound profiles suggests early involvement of the dorsal root ganglia, highlighting an underestimated source of (sensory) ataxia in SCA3. Longitudinal studies are warranted to validate the biomarker potential of these measures. © 2026 The Author(s).
