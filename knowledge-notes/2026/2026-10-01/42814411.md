# 血漿高密度脂蛋白（HDL）亞型與老年人新發失智症風險之關聯

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

在UKB世代中，NMR測得之小型HDL顆粒濃度與六項脂質濃度（總脂質、三酸甘油酯、磷脂質、游離膽固醇、膽固醇酯、總膽固醇）與較低的新發失智症風險相關；而極大型HDL的相應顆粒及脂質濃度則與較高的新發失智症風險相關。在ARIC世代中，AEX界定之HDL次分層1（較小型HDL）中的總三酸甘油酯、總溶血磷脂醯膽鹼（LPC）與磷脂醯乙醇胺（PE），以及次分層2（較大型HDL）中的三酸甘油酯，皆與較低的新發失智症風險相關。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要中提出推測性解釋，指出NMR測得之小型HDL的關聯可能主要由顆粒濃度所驅動，而極大型HDL的關聯則可能同時受脂質含量與顆粒濃度影響；然而摘要並未提供具體生物學機制之實驗驗證，確切機轉仍待全文及後續研究確認。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

由於本研究為觀察性世代研究，僅能說明HDL亞型及其脂質組成與新發失智症風險之間存在關聯，不能據此推論因果關係，亦不可將此結果視為HDL相關治療或介入可降低失智症風險之療效證據。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究以兩種不同分析技術（NMR vs. AEX-MS）在兩個獨立世代中觀察到HDL亞型與失智症風險的關聯模式具有一定一致性，顯示HDL次類別及其脂質組成可能是值得進一步探討的失智症風險相關標記，但摘要亦明確指出不同測量方法使跨世代比較存在限制。

## 批判（Strengths & Limitations）

摘要明確指出，由於UKB與ARIC採用不同之HDL亞型分類及脂質測量方法，跨世代結果難以直接比較。此外，本研究為觀察性設計，無法確立因果關係；ARIC樣本數僅147人，相對UKB之23,790人明顯較小，可能影響結果穩定性與外推性。摘要未提供具體隨訪時間、失智症診斷方式細節及完整共變項清單，亦未說明HDL亞型與脂質組成變化之生物學機制，相關限制與機轉尚待全文確認。

## PubMed Abstract

INTRODUCTION: Plasma high-density lipoprotein (HDL) varies in size. We examined the associations between HDL subclasses and their lipid composition and incident dementia risk in older adults.

METHODS: We included 23,790 United Kingdom (UK) Biobank (UKB) and 147 Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities (ARIC) older adults free of dementia at baseline. HDL subclasses and their lipid composition were characterized using two distinct approaches: Nightingale nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy in UKB and anion-exchange chromatography followed by mass spectrometry-based lipidomics of physically isolated HDL subfractions (AEX-MS) in ARIC. Cox regression models were used to evaluate the associations, adjusting for covariates.

RESULTS: In the UKB, Nightingale NMR-derived plasma small and very large HDL particle and six lipid concentrations (total lipids, triglycerides [TG], phospholipids, free cholesterol, cholesterol ester [CE], total cholesterol) were associated with lower and higher risks of incident dementia, respectively. These associations were likely driven mainly by particle concentration in NMR-derived small HDL and by both lipid and lipid concentrations in NMR-derived very large HDL. In the ARIC, total TG in AEX-defined HDL subfraction 1 (e.g., smaller HDL), total lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC) and phosphatidylethanolamine (PE), and TG in AEX-defined subfraction 2 (e.g., larger HDL) were associated with a lower incident dementia risk.

DISCUSSION: Although different HDL subclass characterization and lipid measurement methods made it difficult to compare results across cohorts, HDL subclasses and their lipid compositions were associated with the risk of incident dementia in both cohorts.
