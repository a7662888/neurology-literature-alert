# 腦類澱粉血管病變（CAA）與阿茲海默症（AD）之中央旁區Tau分布比較

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

CAA患者之整體與中央旁tau SUVR高於對照組，但低於AD患者。CAA患者之中央旁/整體tau比值顯著高於AD（0.93±0.07 vs 0.82±0.08，p<0.001）。以0.90為切點，在推導世代敏感度68.9%、特異度87.1%（AUC=0.836）；在驗證世代敏感度63.1%、特異度94.7%（AUC=0.844）。在CAA患者中，中央旁tau沉積與局部類澱粉沉積相關（係數0.680，p<0.001），亦與白質高訊號體積相關（係數0.001，p=0.040）。空間分布上，tau沉積先出現於頂枕葉皮質，後才涉及內側皮質。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Differentiating cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA) from Alzheimer disease (AD) is essential for prognosis and clinical management. However, pathophysiology-based molecular biomarkers are lacking. Tau deposition may offer discriminative signals: It occurs late in primary cortices including pericentral cortex in AD but may emerge earlier and more diffusely in CAA. We investigated whether a pericentral-predominant pattern of tau deposition distinguishes CAA from AD and characterize its cortical spatial distribution and contributing factors in CAA.

METHODS: We consecutively enrolled healthy controls and patients with CAA and AD with amyloid (18F-florbetapir) and tau (18F-florzolotau) PET. We compared pericentral tau-PET retention (both standardized uptake value ratios [SUVRs] and regional-to-global ratios) between CAA, AD, and controls and identified optimal cutoffs differentiating CAA from AD. We validated the discriminative performance using 18F-flortaucipir tau-PET in an external cohort. We assessed correlates of tau deposition in multivariable regression analyses and analyzed hierarchical patterns of tau involvement using conditional probability analyses in CAA.

RESULTS: The derivation cohort included 22 controls, 80 CAA, and 64 AD (mean age 60.1, 68.4, and 66.3 years; 50.0%, 38.8%, and 50.0% female). The validation cohort included 65 CAA and 19 AD (mean age 74.1 and 73.6 years; 53.8% and 68.4% female). Patients with CAA showed higher global and pericentral tau SUVRs compared with controls but lower than AD. However, pericentral-to-global tau ratios were significantly higher in CAA than AD (0.93 ± 0.07 vs 0.82 ± 0.08, p < 0.001). A cutoff of 0.90 yielded 68.9% (95% CI 58.6-79.6) sensitivity and 87.1% (95% CI 79.1-96.1) specificity in the derivation cohort (AUC = 0.836, 95% CI 0.768-0.904) and 63.1% (95% CI 54.8-71.7) and 94.7% (95% CI 89.5-100), respectively, in the validation cohort (AUC = 0.844, 95% CI 0.747-0.940) for differentiating CAA from AD. In CAA, pericentral tau deposition correlated with regional amyloid deposition (coefficient 0.680, 95% CI 0.489-0.870, p < 0.001) and white matter hyperintensities volume (coefficient 0.001, 95% CI 0.00003-0.001, p = 0.040) and followed parieto-occipital cortices but preceded medial cortex involvement.

DISCUSSION: Higher pericentral-to-global tau ratio differentiates CAA from AD and may reflect spatial distribution patterns potentially influenced by both amyloid and vascular pathology.
