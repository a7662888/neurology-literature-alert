# P2RX7驅動發炎性微膠細胞活化與具致病性細胞外囊泡分泌在Tau蛋白病變中之角色

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

1. 剔除P2rx7基因可減輕PS19小鼠腦萎縮、tau堆積與認知損害。2. 微膠細胞特異性（而非星狀膠細胞特異性）剔除P2rx7可抑制tau蛋白傳播。3. 單細胞定序發現表現P2rx7的「首發反應型微膠細胞狀態」（FRM）。4. P2RX7促使微膠細胞由FRM轉向富含EV生成的發炎性狀態。5. P2RX7增加EV中tau及粒線體蛋白／DNA含量，活化接受細胞的細胞質DNA感應與干擾素訊號。6. 含tau的微膠細胞EV會破壞神經元內吞體／溶酶體完整性，促進tau逃逸與磷酸化。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要指出，P2RX7作為ATP閘控離子通道，調控微膠細胞由FRM狀態轉變為發炎性、富含EV生成機制的狀態；此過程增加EV中tau蛋白及粒線體蛋白／DNA的裝載，活化接受此EV之微膠細胞內的細胞質DNA感應與干擾素訊號路徑，並使含tau的EV破壞神經元內吞體／溶酶體完整性，促成tau蛋白逃逸與磷酸化，形成P2RX7-EV軸介導的tau病理擴散機制。

## PubMed Abstract

P2X purinoreceptor 7 (P2RX7), an ATP-gated ion channel, contributes to Alzheimer's disease (AD) progression, but its molecular mechanisms remain unclear. Here, we show that genetic disruption of P2rx7 mitigates brain atrophy, tau accumulation, and cognitive impairment in PS19 mice. Microglia-specific, but not astrocyte-specific, deletion of P2rx7 suppresses tau propagation. Single-cell RNA sequencing identifies a previously unrecognized first-responding microglial state (FRM) with prominent P2rx7 expression. P2RX7 promotes the transition from FRM to an inflammatory microglial state enriched in extracellular vesicle (EV) biogenesis. P2RX7 increases EV loading of tau and of mitochondrial proteins and DNA, which activate cytosolic DNA sensing and interferon signaling in recipient microglia. Tau-containing microglial EVs disrupt neuronal endolysosomal integrity and facilitate endosomal tau escape and phosphorylation. Thus, P2RX7 regulates the FRM branch point, driving inflammatory microglial transition and EV-mediated spread of tau and toxic mitochondrial molecules and highlighting the P2RX7-EV axis as a therapeutic target in AD.
