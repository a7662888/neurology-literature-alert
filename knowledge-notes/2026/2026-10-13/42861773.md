# 星狀膠細胞SMN功能恢復與Forskolin治療可改善脊髓性肌肉萎縮症之突觸病理

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

1. SMA星狀膠細胞呈現內在性偽足肌動蛋白缺陷，包括肌動蛋白相關細胞表面N-糖蛋白量低、偽足密度降低、肌動蛋白重塑刺激後CDC42-GTP濃度下降，以及磷酸化ezrin、radixin、moesin（pERM）蛋白顯著減少。2. 在SMA運動神經元與星狀膠細胞共培養模型中，觀察到突觸數量、肌動蛋白相關synapsin I表現，以及PAP相關pERM蛋白表現同時下降。3. SMN1基因治療合併forskolin處理後，SMA星狀膠細胞分支與偽足密度增加，SMA運動神經元突觸形成與功能亦有改善現象。

## PubMed Abstract

OBJECTIVE: Early phenotypic presentation of central afferent synapse dysfunction within patients with spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) represents a key target for therapeutic development; however, the underlying disease mechanisms remain unresolved. By further investigating motor neuron synapse and peri-synaptic astrocyte process (PAP) defects in human SMA disease pathology, we aimed to develop a novel therapeutic approach to improve motor neuron function.

METHODS: We utilized healthy subjects and patients with SMA induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived cultures to assess phenotypes occurring intrinsically within astrocyte processes (monocultures) and at the PAP-synaptic interface (co-cultures), respectively, using cell surface capture mass spectrometry proteomics, confocal and super resolution microscopy, synaptogliosome (SG) isolation, and electrophysiology.

RESULTS: SMA astrocytes demonstrated intrinsic filopodia actin defects featuring low abundance of actin-associated cell surface N-glycoproteins, decreased filopodia density and CDC42-GTP levels after actin remodeling stimulation, and significantly reduced phosphorylated ezrin, radixin, and moesin (pERM) proteins. Within the SMA motor neuron and astrocyte co-cultures, we observed concurrent decreases in synapse number, actin-associated synapsin I expression, and PAP-associated expression of pERM proteins. Our dual treatment paradigm using SMN1 gene therapy combined with forskolin treatment to stimulate actin dynamics via increasing cyclic adenosine monophosphate levels resulted in increased branching and filopodia density of SMA astrocytes, and improved synapse formation and function of SMA motor neurons.

INTERPRETATION: We define actin-associated defects impacting astrocyte filopodia dynamics and motor neuron synaptic function in SMA. This shaped our therapeutic approach targeted to SMN-dependent and actin-related disease mechanisms, which overall demonstrated improvements in motor neuron function and disease pathology. ANN NEUROL 2026.
