## 核心發現（Key Findings）

跨異質性協定之MBI研究中，觀察到注意力與再認記憶領域有輕微且具領域特異性之改善；機轉相關資料（雖數量有限）顯示：海馬迴與後扣帶迴間連結增強、大範圍注意力與控制網路內協調性提升、注意力與編碼相關腦反應出現毫秒級時間變化，以及壓力相關生物標記（毛髮皮質醇與皮質酮）降低。

## 機制（Mechanism）

作者提出一個尚屬假說性質、依時間順序排列且具劑量依賴特性的多層次模型：正念練習可能先強化注意力、衝突監控與壓力調節功能，進而可能促進記憶相關網路（與預設模式網路樞紐有關）與額頂葉執行控制網路間的溝通；作者推測，若持續練習，此類功能性變化可能與下游海馬迴及額頂葉區域結構保留有關。惟此機轉模型為摘要作者整合現有有限證據後之推論性提案，並非已獲證實之因果機轉，摘要亦明確指出相關機轉資料有限。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

摘要提出，在此機轉模型架構下，MBI或可作為早期阿茲海默症中疾病修飾治療與生活型態介入之低風險、具機轉基礎的輔助措施，且效果可能因疾病階段而異（例如在輕度認知障礙階段可能強化代償性歷程，在輕度失智階段可能支持日常功能）。惟此為摘要作者基於回顧文獻所做之推論與建議，並非來自對照試驗之療效證實，不應解讀為MBI已證實具有臨床療效。

## 批判（Strengths & Limitations）

## PubMed Abstract

Alzheimer's disease (AD) progresses gradually across early clinical stages, during which compensatory neural mechanisms may remain responsive to behavioral intervention. Mindfulness-based interventions (MBIs) are increasingly studied in mild cognitive impairment (MCI) due to AD and mild AD dementia (early-stage AD), yet most prior reviews emphasize clinical efficacy rather than underlying mechanisms. Here, we review behavioral, neuroimaging, and electrophysiological findings in early-stage AD and propose a multi-level mechanistic model through which mindfulness may influence cognition. Across heterogeneous protocols, MBIs show modest, domain-specific benefits, most consistently in attention and recognition memory. Mechanistic data, though limited, converge on strengthened connectivity between hippocampal and posterior cingulate regions, improved coordination within large-scale attention and control networks, millisecond-scale changes in attention- and encoding-related brain responses, and reduced stress-related markers (i.e., hair cortisol and cortisone). We integrate these findings into a temporally ordered, dose-dependent model in which mindfulness practice may strengthen attention, conflict monitoring, and stress regulation, potentially enhancing communication between memory systems (related to default mode network hubs) and frontoparietal executive control networks. We propose that with sustained practice, such functional changes may contribute to downstream structural preservation in hippocampal and frontoparietal regions. Within this model, MBIs may serve as low-risk, mechanistically informed adjuncts to disease-modifying and lifestyle interventions in early-stage AD. Effects likely vary by stage, from augmenting compensatory processes in MCI to supporting everyday function in mild AD dementia. Future longitudinal trials should incorporate biomarker-confirmed diagnoses, higher practice doses, standardized cognitive outcomes, and multimodal neural assessments.
