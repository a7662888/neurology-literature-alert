# TGF-β 抑制可減緩進行性核上性麻痺患者來源類器官中的 Tau 堆積與神經退化

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

未處理之PSP來源類器官在第90天時，磷酸化tau（Ser396、Thr181、AT8位點）較健康對照類器官增加，同時觀察到多巴胺神經元減少（TH降低、NfL增加）、星狀膠質細胞反應性增強（GFAP增加），以及PSP特徵性tau沉積。給予TGF-β受體I抑制劑之PSP類器官中，tau過度磷酸化、神經退化及星狀膠質細胞反應性皆明顯減少；相反地，給予TGF-β則使這些病理特徵加劇。健康對照類器官暴露於各處理條件下未觀察到毒性反應。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要未提供詳細分子機制說明TGF-β訊號如何導致tau病理與神經退化，僅觀察到抑制TGF-β受體I訊號與tau過度磷酸化及神經退化減少相關，而外加TGF-β則與病理加劇相關，確切下游路徑尚待全文確認。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

本研究屬於體外患者來源類器官模型之臨床前探索性研究，並非人體臨床試驗，因此僅能說明TGF-β抑制在此類器官模型中與PSP相關分子病理減少有關聯，不得解讀為已證實對PSP患者具有療效或可作為臨床治療依據。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究結果支持以患者來源類器官作為模型，探討TGF-β訊號在PSP致病過程中的角色，並為未來進一步以TGF-β為標的之治療策略提供研究基礎，惟是否能延伸至人體臨床試驗仍需後續研究驗證。

## 教學應用（Teaching Application）

可作為教學案例，說明如何利用誘導多功能幹細胞衍生之腦類器官模型探討神經退化性疾病（如4-repeat tauopathy）的分子機制，以及如何透過藥理介入實驗設計（抑制劑 vs. 外加配體 vs. 未處理對照）驗證訊號路徑與病理表現之關聯性，並強調體外模型結果與臨床療效證據之區別。

## 批判（Strengths & Limitations）

本研究樣本數極小，僅來自兩名PSP患者及兩名健康對照者之誘導多功能幹細胞，建構96個類器官進行分組實驗，樣本代表性有限。研究為體外類器官模型，無法完全模擬人體腦部完整微環境與長期病程，亦未提供活體動物或人體臨床試驗數據。摘要未提供TGF-β訊號導致tau病理之詳細分子機制，亦未說明類器官模型的具體限制與外推至臨床情境的適用性，尚待全文及後續研究確認。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP) is a devastating 4-repeat tauopathy with no approved disease-modifying treatments. Recent evidence showed increased transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β) levels in PSP patient-derived ventral midbrain organoids (vMOs) and serum neuronally derived exosomes.

OBJECTIVE: This study investigated whether TGF-β pharmacological inhibition could mitigate PSP pathology in vMOs.

METHODS: Ninety-six vMOs were generated from induced pluripotent stem cells from two PSP-Richardson's syndrome patients and two healthy control donors (HCs). From day 60 to day 90, PSP organoids were (1) exposed to TGF-β receptor I inhibitor (SB431542); (2) exposed to recombinant TGF-β1, TGF-β2, and TGF-β3; or (3) untreated. At day 90, several tau species were assessed by immunoblotting; neurodegeneration was evaluated through TUNEL assay and immunofluorescence (tyrosine hydroxylase [TH], neurofilament light chain [NfL]); astrocytic activation (glial fibrillary acidic protein [GFAP]) marker was evaluated by immunofluorescence, and histopathological hallmarks were evaluated by immunohistochemistry. An HC-derived organoid subgroup was exposed to the treatment conditions to assess potential toxicity.

RESULTS: At day 90, untreated PSP-derived vMOs exhibited increased phosphorylated tau levels (Ser396, Thr181, Ser202/Ser205 [AT8]) compared with HC-derived vMOs, dopaminergic neuron loss (reduced TH, increased NfL), and astrocytic reactivity (increased GFAP), along with PSP characteristic tau deposition. Tau hyperphosphorylation, neurodegeneration, and astrocytic reactivity were all markedly reduced in PSP-derived vMOs treated with TGF-β receptor I inhibitor. Conversely, exposure to TGF-β exacerbated these features. No toxic effects were observed in HC vMOs exposed to treatment conditions.

CONCLUSIONS: Pharmacological inhibition of TGF-β signaling prevented key PSP molecular alterations in patient-derived organoids. These findings support further research and pave the way for future investigation of TGF-β-targeting strategies in clinical trials in patients with PSP. © 2026 The Author(s).
