# 東北醫療巨量生物資料庫世代中以篩檢為基礎之認知功能障礙操作型定義比較：聚焦數位參與度

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

自述失智症者之MMSE與MoCA-J分數較低。既有相關因子（較低教育程度、高血壓、憂鬱症狀、聽力困難）至少在一種分類方式下觀察到與預期方向一致之關聯。研究共辨識出106項在三種分類方式下皆達統計顯著之相關因子。在自述失智症族群中，觀察到未使用數位裝置與較高之勝算比相關（完全校正後OR最高達2.97，95% CI: 1.42-6.18），而使用智慧型手機則與較低勝算比相關（OR=0.47，95% CI: 0.26-0.86）；此關聯方向在以篩檢為基礎之認知功能障礙分類中亦呈一致趨勢。

## PubMed Abstract

BackgroundDementia prevention is a global health priority, yet most large-scale population studies come from Western settings. Japan, a rapidly aging society, provides a unique non-Western epidemiological context for characterizing cognitive decline.ObjectiveTo assess whether the Tohoku Medical Megabank (TMM) cohorts replicate established dementia-related associations, and to identify correlates that are statistically significant across three prespecified operational classifications.MethodsWe analyzed data from >81,000 participants in the TMM cohorts. Participants were classified using (i) self-reported physician-diagnosed dementia and screening-defined impairment using (ii) Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) ≤ 27 and (iii) Japanese version of the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA-J) ≤ 25. Associations with 14 established dementia-related correlates and >3,800 exploratory variables were assessed using false-discovery-rate control, semantic clustering, and sequential multivariable logistic regression.ResultsParticipants with self-reported dementia had lower MMSE and MoCA-J scores. Established correlates replicated in expected directions for at least one classification, including lower education, hypertension, depressive symptoms, and hearing difficulty. We identified 106 significant correlates across all three classifications; for self-reported dementia, digital device non-use was associated with higher odds (fully adjusted OR up to 2.97, 95% CI: 1.42-6.18), while smartphone use showed inverse associations (OR = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.26-0.86); directions were consistent for screening-defined impairment.ConclusionsIn this study, leveraging existing cognitive classifications available in TMM cohorts, we found that established dementia-related associations largely replicated and identified correlates across three operational classifications. Digital disengagement emerged as a correlate that may reflect early functional change; given the cross-sectional design, causal inference requires further examination. Longitudinal validation with adjudicated diagnoses is needed.
