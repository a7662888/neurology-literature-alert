# 以電腦視覺步態狀態追蹤巴金森氏症急性治療反應：外部與前瞻性驗證

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

在回溯性世代中，此生物標記能區分治療前後狀態（交叉驗證AUROC為0.88）。在六個外部世代中，於兩個有藥物標記的世代觀察到一致的藥物效應，並於四個有臨床步態評分的世代與臨床步態損傷程度相關（呈負相關，臨床損傷越高、生物標記分數越低）。在前瞻性世代中，此指標保留了藥物與深腦刺激治療前後的有序變化。在相同配對觀察下，此生物標記之標準化反應平均值（SRM）高於MDS-UPDRS 3.10項（內部1.29對0.83；外部0.92對0.54），且偵測到患者內改善方向變化的比例較高（內部100%對68%，外部84%對37%），此優勢在變化量超過最小可偵測變化後趨於一致。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要描述此生物標記之技術衍生流程：以單一攝影機於治療前後拍攝同一患者，逐幀重建無標記全網格3D姿態，再由此推導出一條連續的步態狀態軸，藥物治療與深腦刺激使患者在此軸上由負值移向正值。摘要未提供此生物標記與巴金森氏症病理生理機轉之間的生物學關聯，尚待全文確認。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

此生物標記與臨床步態損傷評分相關，並在配對觀察中顯示出比傳統臨床步態評分更高的反應敏感度與方向性變化偵測率。此為觀察到的關聯與方法學驗證結果，不能解讀為已證實具有治療效果或可直接取代臨床評估之結論。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究為一數位生物標記之外部與前瞻性驗證，顯示以電腦視覺為基礎、不需特殊硬體的步態狀態量測方法具有跨多中心、多大陸可重複性之潛力，為巴金森氏症運動狀態連續量測提供一項可能的可擴展工具，惟仍屬方法學驗證層次，非療效證明。

## 教學應用（Teaching Application）

## PubMed Abstract

Continuous, treatment-responsive measurement of motor state is an unmet need in Parkinson's disease and a broad challenge for digital biomarkers: clinical gait scales are coarse and ordinal, and most digital measures are never validated beyond their derivation setting. We present a digital biomarker derived from full-mesh 3D human-pose reconstruction in standard monocular video, built from high-contrast within-patient treatment transitions and applied to out-of-sample patients. In a retrospective cohort (53 patients) it discriminated treatment states (cross-validated AUROC 0.88) and defined a continuous motor-state axis. Across six independent cohorts (266 subjects, 3,591 walks, eight sites, three continents) it showed consistent medication effects in the two medication-labelled cohorts and tracked clinical gait impairment in the four cohorts carrying clinical gait ratings; in a prospective cohort (21 patients) it preserved ordered transitions for medication and deep brain stimulation. On identical paired observations it was more responsive than the ordinal MDS-UPDRS Part III item 3.10 (standardized response mean 1.29 versus 0.83 internally, 0.92 versus 0.54 externally); its finer resolution also registered directional within-patient improvement more often, an advantage of measurement resolution that reaches parity once change is required to exceed the minimal detectable change, supporting a scalable, generalizable measurement tool. This graphic explains a computer-vision method for tracking acute treatment response in Parkinson's disease using standard monocular video without specialized hardware. It shows three steps: first, a single camera records the same patient before and after therapy; second, a markerless full-mesh 3D pose is reconstructed frame-by-frame; third, a gait-state biomarker is derived, with Levodopa and deep brain stimulation moving patients up the biomarker axis from negative to positive values. Validation data indicate the method was tested across 3,591 walks from 266 subjects at 8 sites on 3 continents. The biomarker correlates negatively with clinical gait impairment scores, showing that higher clinical impairment corresponds to lower biomarker scores. The biomarker detects changes more often than clinical scores, with 100 percent internal and 84 percent external detection rates of within-patient improvement compared to 68 and 37 percent for clinical gait scores, respectively.
