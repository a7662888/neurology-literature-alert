# 肌萎縮性脊髓側索硬化症中持續存在且區域化分布的T細胞免疫反應

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

研究發現：(1) 存在長期存續的鞘內（intrathecal）T細胞clonotypes，以及具有免疫突觸（immunological synapse）特徵的抗原呈現細胞-T細胞複合體，與鞘內抗原呈現現象一致；(2) CSF中較高比例的CD4+效應型、CD4+調節型與CD8+ T細胞與較慢的疾病進展相關；(3) 周邊血液中CD4+效應型與調節型T細胞比例增加則與較快的疾病進展相關；(4) 血液與CSF中共享的T細胞clonotypes呈現不同的轉錄狀態；(5) 在散發性ALS中，CSF中巨細胞病毒（cytomegalovirus, CMV）特異性T細胞頻率及血清抗CMV抗體與較快疾病進展及較短存活期相關。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

本研究觀察到CSF與周邊血液中T細胞亞群分布與疾病進展速度存在關聯，CSF中特定T細胞族群與較慢進展相關，而周邊血液中特定T細胞族群及CMV相關免疫指標與較快進展及較短存活相關。此為觀察性關聯，不代表因果關係，亦不代表任何免疫調節治療之療效證明。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究提供ALS中免疫系統區域化（compartmentalized）動態之初步證據，顯示鞘內與周邊系統性T細胞反應可能分別對應不同的疾病進展模式，為後續探討ALS免疫病理機轉及潛在生物標記提供研究方向。

## PubMed Abstract

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis is a fatal neurodegenerative disease increasingly linked to T cell responses, although the mechanisms driving T cell activation and disease progression remain unclear. Here we used single-cell RNA and T cell receptor sequencing, longitudinal immune profiling and imaging flow cytometry to identify long-lived intrathecal clonotypes and antigen-presenting cell-T cell complexes displaying features of immunological synapse formation, consistent with intrathecal antigen presentation. Higher frequencies of CD4+ effector, CD4+ regulatory and CD8+ T cells in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) were associated with slower disease progression, whereas increased circulating CD4+ effector and regulatory T cells were associated with faster progression. T cell clonotypes shared between blood and CSF exhibited distinct transcriptional states. Finally, frequencies of cytomegalovirus-specific T cells in CSF and anticytomegalovirus serum antibodies were correlated with faster progression and shorter survival in sporadic amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. These findings reveal compartmentalized immune dynamics, largely contrasting protective intrathecal responses with harmful systemic activation.
