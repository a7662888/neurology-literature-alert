## 核心發現（Key Findings）

PRL相較於非PRL病灶，在病灶內及周邊皆顯示更明顯的微結構異常。PCA顯示存在一個主要的連續性損傷維度，據此分群出可重複的「低損傷」與「高損傷」兩種PRL類型。高損傷型PRL與較高失能程度相關（β=0.43，p=0.035）及較低BPF相關（β=-0.25，p=0.002）；低損傷型PRL則與較高sNfL相關（β=0.35，p=0.010）。縱貫性分析顯示，低損傷型PRL與失能程度較陡峭的上升（交互作用β=0.12，p=0.023）及sGFAP較陡峭上升相關（交互作用β=0.19，p=0.030），且微結構軌跡較不利。分群結果在縱貫追蹤中大致穩定，且兩類病灶在3年前即已存在的比例相近。

## PubMed Abstract

OBJECTIVE: Paramagnetic rim lesions (PRLs) reflect chronic active lesions in multiple sclerosis (MS), but their microstructural heterogeneity remains unclear. We characterized quantitative magnetic resonance imaging (qMRI) abnormalities within PRLs, identified data-driven PRL profiles, and examined their associations with disability, brain volumetry, and serum biomarkers.

METHODS: In this longitudinal study, 175 people with MS (pwMS) and 105 healthy controls underwent multiparametric qMRI. Clinical/serum follow-up was available for 113 pwMS (median, 4.4 years). Lesional/perilesional qMRI metrics were derived. Principal component analysis (PCA) characterized PRL heterogeneity, and clustering identified PRL profiles. Mixed-effects models assessed associations with disability, brain parenchymal fraction (BPF), serum neurofilament light chain (sNfL), and glial fibrillary acidic protein (sGFAP). Longitudinal and retrospective MRI assessed 2-year cluster stability and the presence of corresponding lesions 3 years earlier, respectively.

RESULTS: PRLs showed greater lesional and perilesional microstructural abnormalities than non-PRLs. PCA identified a dominant continuous damage dimension, along which clustering identified 2 reproducible low- and high-damage profiles. High-damage PRLs were associated with higher disability (β = 0.43; p = 0.035) and lower BPF (β = -0.25; p = 0.002), whereas low-damage PRLs were associated with higher sNfL (β = 0.35; p = 0.010). Longitudinally, low-damage PRLs were associated with steeper increases in disability (βinteraction = 0.12; p = 0.023) and sGFAP (βinteraction = 0.19; p = 0.030), and less favorable microstructural trajectories. Cluster assignments were largely stable longitudinally, and similar proportions of lesions from both clusters were already present 3 years earlier.

INTERPRETATION: PRLs exhibit clinically meaningful heterogeneity along a continuous damage spectrum, with cluster assignments largely stable longitudinally. High-damage PRLs are associated with established neurodegeneration, whereas low-damage PRLs show features of ongoing biological activity and worse clinical evolution, supporting the relevance of PRL heterogeneity for prognostic stratification. ANN NEUROL 2026.
