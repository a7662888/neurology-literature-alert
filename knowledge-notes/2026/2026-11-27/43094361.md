## 核心發現（Key Findings）

1) 五種檢測方法測得的血漿p-tau217均隨時間上升，Aβ42/40則大致穩定；2) Aβ-PET陰性族群中，p-tau217上升與同期Aβ累積有關聯（標準化β=0.40-0.52）；3) Aβ-PET陽性族群中，p-tau217上升則與認知退化的關聯較強（標準化β=0.41-0.56），而與Aβ累積無顯著關聯；4) 在AT-PET子樣本中，p-tau217變化與Aβ累積的關聯僅見於A-T-及部分A+T-組，A+T+組未見此關聯；5) p-tau217對Aβ-PET變異的縱貫性貢獻為20.9-28.8%，橫斷面貢獻為31.8-47.9%；對認知變異的縱貫性貢獻為22.2-30.6%，但橫斷面模型中認知狀態貢獻更大；6) p-tau181與GFAP呈現類似但幅度較小的分期依賴型態。以上均為觀察性關聯，非因果證明。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要作者提出一種分期解讀：p-tau217可能反映早期Aβ相關的tau反應，發生於聚集性tau病理形成之前；一旦Aβ病理確立，p-tau217變化則更與認知退化相關。此為摘要作者對觀察到的關聯模式之解釋性推論，並非經由分子或病理機制實驗直接驗證，確切生物機制摘要未提供，尚待全文確認。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

研究觀察到血漿p-tau217的縱貫性變化在不同疾病階段與Aβ累積或認知退化呈現不同關聯型態，提示縱貫性追蹤p-tau217可能有助於依疾病階段監測病程。惟本研究為觀察性分析，顯示的是關聯而非療效或因果證明，不得解讀為p-tau217已被證實可用於治療決策或診斷確診。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究提供p-tau217等血漿生物標記隨時間變化的縱貫性資料，補足過去以橫斷面研究為主的證據缺口，並比較多種檢測方法（14種檢測、5種p-tau217檢測）在縱貫性追蹤上的表現一致性，為未來研究血漿生物標記在疾病分期監測中的角色提供觀察性基礎，仍需進一步前瞻性驗證研究確認其應用價值。

## PubMed Abstract

Plasma p-tau217 biomarkers support Alzheimer's diagnosis but most evidence remains cross-sectional. Understanding how these biomarkers change over time, whether assays perform similarly, and how such changes relate to Aβ plaque accumulation and cognitive decline is needed to guide patient care. We analyzed 393 cognitively unimpaired and impaired participants from the ADNI-FNIH consortium (mean age 72.7 years; 49% female; 37% Aβ-PET positive) with longitudinal plasma biomarkers across 14 assays, Aβ-PET and cognitive assessments over a mean follow-up of 5.4 years. Plasma p-tau217 was measured across five assays, and Aβ42/40, p-tau181, GFAP and NfL were examined in secondary analyses. Linear mixed-effects models estimated individual rates of change for plasma biomarkers, Aβ-PET Centiloids, and cognitive performance. Linear regressions related concurrent rates of change in plasma biomarkers, Centiloids and cognition in the whole group, by Aβ-PET status, and by Aβ and tau (AT) PET status in a subsample of 272 participants. For comparison, analogous cross-sectional analyses examined associations between plasma biomarker levels and baseline Centiloids or cognitive performance. Finally, semipartial R² values were calculated across all models to quantify the relative variance explained by plasma biomarker and other covariates. Plasma p-tau217 levels increased across all five assays (standardized β = 0.06-0.08, P < 0.001), whereas Aβ42/40 was largely stable. Among Aβ-PET negative individuals, greater p-tau217 increases were consistently associated with concurrent Aβ accumulation (standardized β = 0.40-0.52, P < 0.001), but not in Aβ-PET positive individuals. Conversely, greater p-tau217 increases were most strongly associated with cognitive decline in Aβ-PET positive individuals (standardized β = 0.41-0.56, P < 0.001). In the tau-PET subsample, p-tau217 change was associated with Aβ accumulation in A-T- and, to a lesser extent, A+T- individuals, but not in A+T+ individuals. P-tau217 was the largest contributor to Aβ-PET variance in longitudinal (20.9-28.8%) and cross-sectional (31.8-47.9%) models. For cognition, however, p-tau217 change was the largest contributor in longitudinal models (22.2-30.6%), whereas cognitive status contributed more than p-tau217 cross-sectionally. Although of lower magnitude than for p-tau217, rates of change in p-tau181 and GFAP showed similar stage-dependent associations. In earlier stages, longitudinal changes in p-tau217 paralleled ongoing Aβ accumulation, whereas once Aβ pathology was established, p-tau217 change was more closely associated with cognitive decline than with further Aβ accumulation. These findings indicate that p-tau217 reflects an early Aβ-associated tau response before aggregated tau pathology, supporting the value of longitudinal p-tau217 for stage-dependent disease monitoring.
