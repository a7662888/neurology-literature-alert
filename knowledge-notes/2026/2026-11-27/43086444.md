## 核心發現（Key Findings）

摘要指出，在發育過程中，當 Pcdh 基因被活化時，會形成一個包含 Pcdh 基因的緊密異染色質結構域；Pcdh 的轉錄發生於此緊密結構域內，並可穿讀（read through）下游基因；遠端增強子必須進入此結構域才能與目標啟動子接觸。小鼠的基因操作實驗顯示，兩種相互對立的基因體折疊力量似乎共同作用，以建立 Pcdh 基因座結構並促進 Pcdh 的隨機表現。

## PubMed Abstract

Heterochromatin typically represses genes1,2, yet some genes paradoxically require it for activation3-7. How can the same chromatin environment both silence and promote transcription? Here we address this question by interrogating the clustered protocadherin (Pcdh) locus, where subsets of genes are stochastically expressed while most remain silent8. In neurons, stochastic and combinatorial expression of Pcdh genes generates unique cell-surface barcodes required for self-non-self discrimination and neural circuit assembly8. By combining RNA labelling of Pcdh genes and neurodevelopmental cell types with single-molecule chromatin tracing and stochastic optical reconstruction microscopy, we find that a compact heterochromatin domain containing Pcdh genes forms as these genes become activated during development. Pcdh transcription occurs within the compact domain and reads through downstream genes, and distal enhancers must access the domain to engage target promoters. Genetic perturbations in mice suggest that opposing genome folding forces cooperate to establish Pcdh locus architecture and promote stochastic Pcdh expression. Specifically, while DNA loop extrusion allows enhancers to breach heterochromatin and find target promoters, heterochromatin-driven compartmentalization supports an expanded enhancer-promoter search space. These findings shed light on a process foundational to the development of vertebrate nervous systems and suggest a mechanism for how transcription can occur within seemingly silent chromatin environments.
