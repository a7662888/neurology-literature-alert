# 以三維人類視網膜模型揭示溶酶體儲積疾病中光受體喪失的非細胞自主性、非神經元性機制

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

1. CLN3Δex7-8突變導致酸性神經醯胺酶（AC）與鞘胺醇-1-磷酸（S1P）濃度降低，並伴隨光受體外節喪失。2. 比較對照組與CLN3突變RPE單一培養及hPSC-RO-RPE模型後，研究者指出AC所調控的溶酶體鞘脂代謝異常，在CLN3疾病病理機轉中扮演致病角色。3. 研究顯示RPE功能異常在RPE細胞本身具有細胞自主性（即RPE內部異常源自CLN3突變本身），並促成光受體外節喪失／視網膜退化，此一光受體喪失過程相對於光受體本身而言則屬於非細胞自主性、非神經元起源。4. 在CLN3迷你豬模型與兩名CLN3疾病病患捐贈眼球中，觀察到與類器官模型一致的分子與結構變化。5. 兩名CLN3疾病病患之高解析度活體視網膜影像顯示，早期疾病階段RPE自體螢光有減少的現象。6. 以rhAC治療後，CLN3疾病RO-RPE模型與CLN3迷你豬眼球中的光受體退化情形獲得改善。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要指出，CLN3Δex7-8突變造成RPE細胞內酸性神經醯胺酶活性降低，進而影響鞘胺醇-1-磷酸等溶酶體鞘脂代謝路徑；此代謝異常源自RPE細胞本身（細胞自主性於RPE），但導致的光受體外節喪失則屬於非細胞自主性、非神經元機制，即光受體喪失並非源於光受體本身缺陷，而是由鄰近RPE功能障礙所觸發。詳細的分子傳遞機制摘要未完整說明，尚待全文確認。

## PubMed Abstract

Disruption of the photoreceptor-retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) interface, with loss of photoreceptor outer segments (POSs) in the retina, is a pathological hallmark of several neurodegenerative and retinal diseases, including lysosomal storage disorders like juvenile neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis (CLN3) disease. However, in vitro stem cell models that enable investigation of the photoreceptor-RPE interface are lacking. Here, we developed a 3D human pluripotent stem cell-derived retina organoid-RPE (hPSC-RO-RPE) model to investigate the photoreceptor-RPE interface in healthy and diseased tissues. Using this 3D hPSC-RO-RPE retina model, we showed that the most common disease-causing CLN3 mutation (CLN3Δex7-8) leads to reduced levels of acid ceramidase (AC), sphingosine 1 phosphate, and POS loss. Furthermore, by analyzing control versus CLN3 mutant (CLN3Δex7-8) hPSC-derived RPE monoculture and hPSC-RO-RPE model, (i) we identified a pathogenic role of AC-mediated lysosomal sphingolipid metabolism in promoting CLN3 disease pathobiology, and (ii) we showed a cell autonomous role of the RPE dysfunction in promoting POS loss/retina degeneration in CLN3 disease. We validated the molecular and structural changes observed in the CLN3Δex7-8 hPSC-RO-RPE model in the CLN3 miniswine model (CLN3Δex7-8) and donor eyes from two patients with CLN3 disease. High-resolution retinal imaging of the living eye in two patients with CLN3 disease suggested decreased RPE autofluorescence in early-stage CLN3 disease. Treatment with recombinant human acid ceramidase (rhAC) ameliorated photoreceptor degeneration in both the CLN3 disease RO-RPE model and CLN3 miniswine eyes. These findings suggest that rhAC could be a therapeutic approach for retinal degeneration in CLN3 disease.
