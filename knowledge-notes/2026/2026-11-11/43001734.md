## 核心發現（Key Findings）

Pelacarsen顯著降低血清脂蛋白(a)濃度（12個月時由基線中位數236 nmol/L降至64.4 nmol/L），但主要心血管不良事件（心血管死亡、非致命性心肌梗塞、非致命性腦中風或緊急血管重建）發生率與安慰劑組無統計學差異（12.3% vs. 12.1%，HR 1.02，P=0.76）。在高脂蛋白(a)亞組（≥90 mg/dL）結果亦相似（HR 1.00，P=0.99）。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

此試驗顯示，儘管pelacarsen能有效降低脂蛋白(a)濃度，但在中位追蹤4.7年期間，並未觀察到其降低主要心血管事件風險的療效證據。此結果與「降低脂蛋白(a)即可降低心血管風險」之假設不一致。

## 研究意義（Research Relevance）

本研究提供了大型隨機對照試驗的直接證據，顯示降低脂蛋白(a)濃度本身未必轉化為臨床心血管效益，對未來脂蛋白(a)標靶治療藥物之療效評估具參考價值。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Pelacarsen, an antisense oligonucleotide, reduces serum levels of lipoprotein(a). However, the effect of pelacarsen on cardiovascular outcomes remains uncertain.

METHODS: We conducted an international, randomized, placebo-controlled trial involving patients with preexisting cardiovascular disease and a lipoprotein(a) level of at least 70 mg per deciliter. Patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive subcutaneous pelacarsen (80 mg) administered monthly or matching placebo. The primary end point was a major adverse cardiovascular event, defined as a composite of death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, nonfatal stroke, or urgent coronary revascularization.

RESULTS: A total of 8350 patients underwent randomization, with 8322 included in the efficacy analysis; 4157 patients were assigned to receive pelacarsen, and 4165 to receive placebo. The mean age of the patients was 59.7 years, and 27.0% were women. The median lipoprotein(a) level at baseline was 236 nmol per liter. At 12 months, the lipoprotein(a) level was 64.4 nmol per liter in the pelacarsen group and 233.4 nmol per liter in the placebo group. The median follow-up was 4.7 years. A primary end-point event occurred in 511 patients (12.3%) in the pelacarsen group and in 503 (12.1%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.02, 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.90 to 1.15, P = 0.76). Among patients with a baseline lipoprotein(a) level of at least 90 mg per deciliter, a primary end-point event occurred in 402 of 3266 patients (12.3%) in the pelacarsen group and in 403 of 3274 (12.3%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.00; 95% CI, 0.87 to 1.15; P = 0.99). Serious adverse events occurred in 1553 of 4166 patients (37.3%) who received pelacarsen and in 1709 of 4154 (41.1%) who received placebo.

CONCLUSIONS: Pelacarsen did not result in a lower risk of major adverse cardiovascular events than placebo at a median follow-up of 4.7 years.
