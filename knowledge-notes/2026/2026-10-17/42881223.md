1. dFBN神經元產生的慢波活動與睡眠具有因果關聯（在此果蠅模型中）。2. dFBN族群含雙穩態細胞，膜電位於DOWN與UP態間振盪，此振盪源自兩個半中心透過抑制性麩胺酸突觸的半球間競爭。3. 限制於dFBN的麩胺酸釋放干預會破壞SWA及基礎與反彈性睡眠。4. 以光遺傳學方式重現SWA可誘發睡眠。5. dFBN在整個睡眠—清醒週期中持續產生SWA，並與促醒多巴胺神經元存在相互拮抗。6. SWA強度會依睡眠史而調整，涉及興奮性提升與恆定性突觸抑制。

## PubMed Abstract

Changes in oscillatory neural dynamics reflect-but may not necessarily determine-transitions between sleep stages and variations in sleep pressure. Here we show that sleep-control neurons innervating the dorsal fan-shaped body of Drosophila (dFBNs) produce slow-wave activity (SWA) that is causally linked to sleep. The dFBN ensemble contains bistable cells whose membrane voltages oscillate between hyperpolarized DOWN and depolarized UP states. The oscillations rely on interhemispheric competition of two half-centers connected by inhibitory glutamatergic synapses. dFBN-restricted interference with glutamate release disrupts SWA and baseline as well as rebound sleep, whereas the optogenetic replay of SWA induces sleep. dFBNs generate SWA throughout the sleep-wake cycle (despite mutual antagonism with arousing dopaminergic neurons) and adjust its power to sleep need via sleep history-dependent increases in excitability and homeostatic synaptic depression. The oscillatory format permits a continuous encoding of sleep pressure but requires mechanisms that convert the amplitude-modulated signal into binary sleep-wake states.
