# 一種對老化敏感的分泌型磷脂酶可提供神經保護並促進認知韌性

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

小鼠實驗顯示，在 DGC 中剔除 Pla2g2f 會加劇老化相關的病理變化，包括突觸喪失、發炎性微膠細胞增加、反應性星形膠質增生、神經新生受損、脂質失調、脂滴堆積，以及海馬迴依賴性記憶喪失。相反地，在老化過程中於 DGC 中增加 Pla2g2f 表現，足以預防上述病理變化並保護記憶功能，同時可預先改變認知衰退的軌跡。催化位點突變的 PLA2G2F 則無法促進脂滴移動，也無法降低反應性膠質增生與發炎性微膠細胞反應。此外，神經元來源的 PLA2G2F 可透過細胞間訊號傳遞，降低人類 iPSC 衍生微膠細胞中的脂滴與膽固醇酯含量。

## PubMed Abstract

Cognitive reserve theory posits that compensatory mechanisms during aging stave off cognitive decline and lower risk for Alzheimer's disease. Here, in a screen for hippocampal dentate granule cell (DGC) synapse loss-induced factors in mice, we identified a secreted phospholipase, Pla2g2f, whose expression increases in DGCs during aging and is associated with cognitive resilience in humans. Pla2g2f deletion in DGCs exacerbates aging-associated pathophysiological changes, including synapse loss, inflammatory microglia, reactive astrogliosis, impaired neurogenesis, lipid dysregulation, lipid droplet accumulation and hippocampal-dependent memory loss. Conversely, boosting Pla2g2f in DGCs during aging is sufficient to prevent these pathophysiological alterations in hippocampal-cortical circuits, protect memory and prophylactically modify trajectory of cognitive decline. Mutagenesis of the catalytic site of PLA2G2F prevented lipid droplet mobilization and reduction of reactive gliosis and inflammatory microglia. Neuronal PLA2G2F mediates intercellular signaling to decrease lipid droplet and cholesterol ester load in human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived microglia. Our findings implicate PLA2G2F as a compensatory neuroprotective broadcast signal that maintains lipid homeostasis to counteract aging-associated cognitive decline.
