## PubMed Abstract

It is unclear how serotonin 2A receptors (5-HT2ARs) in cortical layer 5 pyramidal neurons differentially contribute to psilocybin-induced hallucinations versus neuroplasticity. Here we show that psilocybin promotes synapse formation and maturation while accelerating the elimination of pre-existing synapses. Cell type-specific manipulation further demonstrated that 5-HT2AR signaling in cortical layer 5 pyramidal neurons is necessary and sufficient for psilocybin-induced synaptic remodeling but dispensable for the head-twitch response, a rodent proxy of hallucination.
