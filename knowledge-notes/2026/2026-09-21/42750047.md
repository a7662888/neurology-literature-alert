# 靶向人類SNCA 3'UTR反義寡核苷酸在BAC-hSNCA大鼠模型中的腦區解析藥效學研究

## 核心發現（Key Findings）

SNCA ASO3.0以區域漸進方式降低人類SNCA mRNA及多種α-synuclein蛋白種類，效果在嗅球與紋狀體最強。RNA-seq顯示不同腦區有各自不同的轉錄反應，包括嗅球中突觸與代謝路徑之變化，以及紋狀體與中腦膠質細胞相關基因表現富集，但GFAP與Iba1蛋白層級未見可偵測變化。細胞因子分析顯示部分分析物有選擇性調節，磷酸化激酶分析顯示AKT1S1/PRAS40磷酸化降低。紋狀體多巴胺含量未改變，但紋狀體酪胺酸羥化酶（TH）含量增加。SNCA ASO3.0使動物運動活動增加，並改善探索行為與部分嗅覺辨別測量指標。

## 機制（Mechanism）

摘要描述該ASO靶向人類SNCA 3'UTR以降低人類SNCA mRNA及其蛋白產物表現，並伴隨腦區特異性轉錄反應（如突觸、代謝與免疫/膠質相關基因表現變化）及AKT1S1/PRAS40磷酸化降低；然而摘要未說明此分子變化如何具體導致觀察到的行為改變之上游或下游因果路徑，此部分尚待全文確認。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

本研究屬於動物（人源化大鼠）模型之藥效學研究，並非人體臨床試驗，結果顯示部分抑制人類α-synuclein表現在此模型中與特定腦區分子變化及早期行為改變相關，但不能據此推論對人類巴金森氏症具有療效或因果性的疾病修飾作用，僅可視為概念驗證性資料。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Alpha-synuclein accumulation contributes to Parkinson's disease (PD), and lowering α-synuclein expression is a candidate disease-modifying approach. The brain-wide pharmacodynamic profile of human-targeting antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) remains incompletely defined in humanized models that preserve the regulatory architecture of the human SNCA locus.

METHODS: BAC-hSNCA rats were treated at an early stage, when human α-synuclein is elevated, but striatal dopamine content remains largely preserved. A single intracerebroventricular dose of an ASO targeting human SNCA 3'UTR (SNCA ASO3.0) was administered, and outcomes were assessed 45 days later by isoform-specific RT-qPCR, immunoblotting of soluble and detergent-insoluble α-synuclein species, bulk RNA-seq across selected regions, multiplex cytokine profiling, phospho-kinase assays, neurotransmitter quantification, and behavioral testing.

RESULTS: SNCA ASO3.0 reduced human SNCA mRNA and multiple α-synuclein protein species in a regionally graded manner, with the strongest effects in the olfactory bulb and striatum. Bulk RNA-seq identified anatomically distinct transcriptional responses, including synaptic and metabolic pathway shifts in the olfactory bulbs and immune-related enrichment within glial signatures in the striatum and midbrain, without detectable changes in protein levels of glial fibrillary acidic protein or ionized calcium-binding adapter molecule 1. Cytokine profiling showed selective modulation of a subset of analytes, and phospho-kinase assays indicated reduced AKT1S1/PRAS40 phosphorylation. Striatal dopamine levels were unchanged, whereas the striatal tyrosine hydroxylase level was increased. SNCA ASO3.0 increased locomotor activity and improved exploratory behavior and selected measures of olfactory discrimination.

CONCLUSIONS: Partial suppression of human α-synuclein in a humanized rat model yields region-specific molecular adaptations accompanied by early behavioral changes at a stage preceding overt dopamine depletion. These data provide a framework for evaluating region-resolved consequences of α-synuclein-lowering interventions in synucleinopathies.
