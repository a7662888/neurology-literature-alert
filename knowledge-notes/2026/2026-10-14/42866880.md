## 核心發現（Key Findings）

統合分析顯示，靜脈血栓溶解治療可改善90天優良功能結果（風險比1.22，95%信賴區間1.16-1.29）與功能獨立（修訂版Rankin量表0-2分，風險比1.12，95%信賴區間1.06-1.18），alteplase與tenecteplase皆對此效益有貢獻；同時血栓溶解治療增加症狀性顱內出血風險（風險比2.58，95%信賴區間1.58-4.22），但對90天死亡率無統計上顯著影響（風險比1.15，95%信賴區間0.97-1.35）。

## 臨床意義（Clinical Relevance）

在經影像篩選之病人中，於傳統4.5小時治療窗之後給予靜脈血栓溶解治療，與功能結果改善有關，且未觀察到90天死亡率顯著增加；惟症狀性顱內出血風險明顯上升，提示臨床應用需權衡功能效益與出血風險，並以影像為基礎（而非僅以時間為基礎）進行病人篩選。

## PubMed Abstract

BACKGROUND: Intravenous thrombolysis is a standard treatment for acute ischemic stroke but has traditionally been limited to 4.5 hours after symptom onset. This time-based restriction may exclude patients with salvageable brain tissue, especially those with wake-up or unknown-onset strokes. Multimodal imaging allows for the identification of viable penumbra beyond traditional treatment windows. This study compiles randomized evidence evaluating intravenous thrombolysis in imaging-selected patients treated after 4.5 hours.

METHODS: We conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis in accordance with Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses guidelines. PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Central were searched through February 2026 for randomized trials comparing intravenous thrombolysis (alteplase or tenecteplase) with best medical therapy in patients presenting beyond 4.5 hours or with wake-up/unknown-onset stroke selected by imaging. The primary outcome was excellent functional outcome at 90 days (modified Rankin Scale score 0-1). Secondary outcomes included functional independence (modified Rankin Scale score 0-2), symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage, and 90-day mortality.

RESULTS: Fourteen trials involving 4192 participants were analyzed. Intravenous thrombolysis improved excellent functional outcomes (modified Rankin Scale score 0-1; risk ratio, 1.22 [95% CI, 1.16-1.29]) and functional independence (modified Rankin Scale score 0-2; risk ratio, 1.12 [95% CI, 1.06-1.18]). Both alteplase and tenecteplase contributed to these benefits. Thrombolysis increased the risk of symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (risk ratio, 2.58 [95% CI, 1.58-4.22]) but did not significantly affect 90-day mortality (risk ratio, 1.15 [95% CI, 0.97-1.35]).

CONCLUSIONS: In patients selected using advanced imaging, intravenous thrombolysis performed after 4.5 hours improves functional outcomes without increasing mortality, supporting a tissue-based rather than strictly time-based approach to treating acute ischemic stroke.
