## PubMed Abstract

Although Alzheimer's disease (AD) has a polygenic architecture, with numerous associated single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) identified, the contribution of structural variants (SVs) is unclear. In this study, using long-read (n = 11) and short-read (n = 3,190) sequencing data from the Chinese population, we show that common SVs contribute to AD heritability beyond common SNPs. We identify an expanded CCG repeat in the FAM193B promoter that shows a genome-wide significant association with increased AD risk (odds ratio = 2.20 per 10-repeat increase, P = 5.5 × 10-13). The expanded FAM193B-CCG repeat increases FAM193B expression and mediates the pleiotropic association between brain FAM193B expression and AD risk. Analyses of postmortem human brains and cultured neuronal cells show that elevated neuronal FAM193B expression is associated with DNA damage and cell death-related pathways. These findings establish the common FAM193B-CCG repeat as an AD risk genetic factor and highlight the contribution of common SVs to AD heritability.
